About Us

People Who Still Sharpen Their Own Chisels

Heartwood Workshop is a small team of makers who believe furniture should be built once, built well and repaired — not replaced.

About us

The Team Behind the Craft

At WoodCraft Studio, we bridge the gap between traditional carpentry and modern structural design. Our team is comprised of seasoned artisans who treat every grain of wood with the respect it deserves.

We specialize in precision engineered furniture, employing traditional hand cut joinery to ensure structural integrity that lasts for generations. From custom sliding door systems to bespoke LED-integrated floating panels, we refuse to cut corners. For us, every joint is a testament to our quality, and every finished piece is a commitment to excellence.

How We Got Here

A Short History of the Workshop

2008

The Early Days (The Solo Era)

Started as a one-man workshop focusing on small, custom projects. Every piece was a personal challenge to refine the craft.

2019

The Family Connection

Joined forces with my father merging his years of traditional carpentry expertise with fresh modern design concepts.

2021

Growing the Bench

Transitioned from a solo venture to a professional team. Invested in a dedicated workshop space to handle larger and more complex projects.

Today

Still Hand Fitting Every Joint

A collaborative studio where tradition meets modern precision. We are now a fully fledged team, yet every project still receives the same hands on attention to detail we started with.

What We Believe

Principles That Slow Us Down On Purpose

Honesty of Material

We show the grain instead of hiding it. If a board has character, the design works with it, not against it.

Joinery Over Glue

Mortise and tenon and dovetail joints where they matter structurally — glue alone doesn't outlive a house move.

Designed For Decades

Every piece is built to be repaired, refinished, and passed on, not replaced when trends shift.
